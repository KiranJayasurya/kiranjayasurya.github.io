01 ABOUT

Observing the Universe
at its most extreme.

I am a researcher in high-energy astrophysics interested in accretion onto compact objects, neutron-star magnetic fields, and the physics of ultraluminous X-ray sources.

My work uses X-ray spectroscopy, timing, and polarimetry to investigate the geometry and physical conditions of accretion flows operating near and above the Eddington limit.
