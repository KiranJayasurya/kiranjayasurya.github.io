01 RESEARCH

Accretion in
extreme environments.

I use high-energy observations to investigate how matter behaves in the strong-gravity and strong-magnetic-field environments around compact objects.

R / 01

Super-Eddington accretion

How radiation pressure, winds, and geometric collimation shape ultraluminous X-ray sources and supercritical accretion flows.

ULXsAccretion physics

R / 02

X-ray spectro-polarimetry

Using polarization measurements to constrain the geometry of accretion columns, scattering regions, and outflows.

IXPEPolarization

R / 03

Neutron-star magnetic fields

Searching for cyclotron resonance scattering features and spectral signatures of strongly magnetized accreting neutron stars.
